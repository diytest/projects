ST-Link Programmer for the STM8-based adapter, and possibly an ATmega8 programmer from AliExpress

can be offered at a markup - can be bought on AliExpress + markup; if shipped together with the adapter, the shipping cost is unlikely to increase - the weight and dimensions are small.

Cables can be offered made to order, or ready-made ones can be purchased 

if it's worthwhile: making soft, lightweight cables for accelerometers and gyroscopes - devices requiring a minimal-weight sensor board and maximum cable lightness and flexibility; a needed length can be cut off from purchased cables for the customer;
a turnkey kit can be made - adapter, cable, shock absorber, enclosure, selected device boards.
All of this is a question of price and whether there is someone to do the assembly and on what terms.
Most likely, it's better for now to limit things to the adapters, and later, possibly, ship kits: adapter + enclosure + device boards with chips or without chips, without dealing with cables at all.
